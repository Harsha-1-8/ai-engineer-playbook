origin story

It started as an R&D side quest at work.

When an AI-assistant pricing change was on the horizon, I started digging into a simple question: can we get the same results from GitHub Copilot while spending fewer tokens, without the quality dropping?

The answers turned out to be habits, not hacks: how much context you hand over, when you start a fresh chat, which model you pick, how you share logs and shape output, and how you verify what comes back. I wrote them up as a short episodic series for fellow developers.

This is the public, interactive remake: the same lessons, rebuilt so you can play with each idea instead of just reading about it.
